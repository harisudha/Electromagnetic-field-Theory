6. Important Relationships

Φ ∝ Q_enc

Flux depends entirely on the enclosed charge.

Φ ∡ Shape

Flux is independent of Gaussian surface geometry.

E ∝ 1/r²

Field intensity drops with the square of distance.

Q_ext = 0 Flux

Charges outside the surface yield net zero flux.

7. Theory

What is Gauss's Law?

Gauss's Law states that the net electric flux passing through any closed surface is equal to the net charge enclosed by that surface divided by the vacuum permittivity (ε₀).


Integral Form & Solid Angle

dΩ = (u_r · n̂ ds) / r² = ds' / (r')²

Φ_E = ∮_S E · n̂ ds = Q_enc / ε₀

Where:

Φ_E = total electric flux (N·m²/C or V·m)

E = electric field vector (N/C)

ds = differential area element on arbitrary surface S

ds' = projected differential area element on concentric sphere S' of radius r'

Q_enc = net electric charge inside the surface (C)

ε₀ = 8.854 × 10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²)
