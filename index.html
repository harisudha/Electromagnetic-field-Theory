About the Course

The course covers electrostatics, magnetostatics, and electromagnetic waves, including coordinate systems, vector calculus, Coulomb's and Biot–Savart's laws, Ampere's law, electric and magnetic fields, energy density, plane waves, and wave propagation in free space, dielectrics, and conductors.
